Your Questions, Answered

Clear Answers to Pricing & Plans.

What's the difference between monthly and annual billing?+
Annual billing offers a significant discount compared to monthly payments, typically saving you up to 20%. You pay for a full year upfront, locking in a lower rate. Monthly billing provides more flexibility but at a slightly higher cost.
Can I change my plan after I subscribe?+
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your BOON plan at any time through your account dashboard. Upgrades are effective immediately and prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
Are there any hidden fees?+
No, we believe in transparent pricing. The price you see is the price you pay. There are no setup fees, cancellation fees, or hidden charges.
What if I need a custom plan for a large organization?+
For larger teams, specific industry needs, or unique requirements, our Enterprise plan offers custom solutions. Please contact our sales team to discuss your needs and receive a personalized quote.
