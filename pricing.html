Your Questions, Answered

We've compiled answers to common inquiries about purchasing BOON products.

What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex) as well as PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay for a seamless checkout experience.
Can I track my order?+
Yes, absolutely! Once your order has shipped, you will receive an email with a tracking number and a link to monitor your package's journey directly to your door.
Do you offer gift wrapping or personalization?+
Currently, we do not offer gift wrapping or personalization services. However, BOON arrives in beautifully designed, eco-friendly packaging perfect for gifting!
What if my BOON product arrives damaged?+
In the rare event that your product arrives damaged, please contact our customer support team immediately with photos. We will arrange a replacement or refund promptly.
Is there a warranty on BOON products?+
Yes, all BOON Productivity Hubs come with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Please refer to our Terms of Service for full details.
Can I purchase BOON products in physical stores?+
Currently, BOON products are exclusively available through our official website to ensure the highest quality and direct customer service.
Are there any discounts for bulk orders?+
For corporate gifting or bulk purchases, please contact our sales team directly through the support page. We'd be happy to discuss special pricing options.
How do I return a product?+
Initiating a return is simple. Visit our support page and fill out the returns form, or email us directly at returns@boon.com within 30 days of receipt.
