Common Questions About Our Pricing

Is the £497 a recurring fee?+
No, the £497 for the Standard Bot Launch is a one-time fee to cover the development and launch of your custom Telegram bot. The Pro Support & Scaling plan is a monthly subscription.
What if I need features not listed in the plans?+
If you require highly specialized features or integrations, our 'Custom Bot Solutions' (see Services page) are designed for this. Contact us, and we'll provide a tailored quote.
Can I upgrade or cancel my Pro Support plan?+
Yes, you can upgrade or cancel your Pro Support & Scaling plan at any time with a 30-day notice. Your bot will continue to function, but without the premium support and advanced features.
Are there any hidden costs?+
No, we believe in complete transparency. The prices listed cover the services described. Any custom work or additional requests would be quoted separately and approved by you beforehand.
