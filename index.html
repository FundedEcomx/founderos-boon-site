BOON is a cloud-based SaaS, so it can be accessed from anywhere in the world with an internet connection. Setup is typically instant once you subscribe, and integrating your platforms takes only a few minutes.
What is your refund policy?+
We offer a full 30-day money-back guarantee. If BOON doesn't meet your expectations within the first 30 days of your subscription, simply contact our support team for a full refund.
Are your integrations secure/certified?+
Yes, security is our top priority. All integrations use industry-standard OAuth 2.0 protocols and encrypted connections. We are fully compliant with GDPR and other data privacy regulations.
What are the core features of BOON?+
BOON offers AI-powered automation for social media, email marketing, content scheduling, and audience engagement. It also includes robust analytics, CRM integration, and a custom strategy builder.
How do I get the most out of BOON?+
We recommend starting with our onboarding guide and exploring the template library. Our dedicated support team and extensive knowledge base are always available to help you optimize your strategy and unlock BOON's full potential.
Can I upgrade/downgrade my plan?+
Absolutely! You can easily upgrade or downgrade your BOON subscription at any time directly from your account dashboard. Changes will be prorated and take effect immediately or at your next billing cycle.
Is BOON suitable for very small teams or agencies?+
BOON is designed primarily for solo creators and small businesses. Our Team Plan accommodates up to 3 users, making it suitable for lean teams. For larger agencies, please contact us for custom solutions.
How do I contact customer support?+
You can reach our customer support team via live chat directly within the BOON application during business hours, or by emailing support@boon.com. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours.
